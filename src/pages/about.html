Our story

From our garage to your table

We're Hummus Y'all, a small family business in North Carolina. We started selling from our home garage, and thanks to your support we've grown. Now we proudly bring hummus to Raleigh, Durham, Apex, Cary and surrounding areas.

Our mission? Spreading the joy of Mediterranean-style hummus to the South and beyond.

It's not a dip. It's a meal.

What sets us apart is our belief that hummus should be enjoyed as a whole meal, not just a dip. We use only the best ingredients, including imported tahini and extra virgin olive oil. Our hummus has no preservatives and is vegan and vegetarian-friendly.

It can be eaten cold, but it's made to be served slightly warm. Thirty seconds in the microwave brings back the original flavor and texture, as if it were just made.

Meet the team
